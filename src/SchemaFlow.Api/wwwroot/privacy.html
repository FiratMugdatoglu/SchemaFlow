Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Zero-Knowledge Local Processing

SchemaFlow operates on a privacy-first, zero-knowledge architecture. When you run SchemaFlow CLI tools or parse migration files locally, your schema definitions, table designs, SQL scripts, and database credentials remain 100% on your machine. We do not transmit, inspect, or store your database structures on our servers.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information needed to provide services:

  • Account Information: When you purchase a Pro or Team license, your billing information and email are processed securely by Lemon Squeezy (our Merchant of Record).
  • Anonymous Telemetry: Minimal anonymous usage telemetry (e.g. CLI version, command invocation counts) to detect bugs and guide improvements. No schema or column contents are ever included.

3. Third-Party Payment Processing

All payments, subscriptions, and invoice generations are securely handled by Lemon Squeezy. We never store credit card numbers or sensitive banking data on SchemaFlow servers.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@schemaflow.ai.
